The Artist

Pratik Gawande

Founder and CEO

Pratik Gawande Photography PGP is an Amravati & Pune-based wedding and pre-wedding photography firm, passionate about capturing love stories in their most authentic and beautiful form.

With years of experience behind the lens, we specialize in documenting the magic of weddings - from the grandest rituals to the quietest, most intimate moments. Whether it's a vibrant pre-wedding shoot, a traditional ceremony, or a dreamy destination wedding, our goal is to create timeless memories that speak of love, emotion, and celebration. Our approach blends candid storytelling with artistic composition, ensuring every frame reflects the unique essence of each couple. Though rooted in Pune, our work knows no boundaries.

We travel across cities and countries to be part of love stories that deserve to be told with heart and honesty. At Pratik Gawande Photography PGP, we don't just take pictures - we freeze emotions, laughter, tears, and joy that last a lifetime. Let us be a part of your big day, and we'll make sure every click tells a story worth cherishing forever.
